Pc controls:

Blue Ball:
A=left
D=right
S=insult
F=ability
space=jump

Red Ball:
Left arrow key=left
Right arrow key=right
Up arrow key=ability
Down arrow key=insult

Misc:
E=turn red ball into AI

Controller controls:

A=jump(only for blue)
B=insult
Y=swap to red ball
X=ability
Left analog stick = movment
